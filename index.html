        How Payo works

        Payo splits group expenses, and the link is the database. The whole trip is compressed into the address you share, so nothing is ever uploaded anywhere.

        1. Create a trip and add what you paid.
        2. Copy link and drop it in the group chat.
        3. Friends open it, add their own expenses and send their link back.
        4. Open or paste any link you get back. Payo merges it: new expenses, edits and deletions combine with no duplicates, in any order.

        At the same table? Press Show QR and friends scan it with their phone camera. Amounts accept math (3*14.50, 120/4). “Mark paid” records a settlement. Your trip lives in three places: this browser, every link you've shared, and any backup file you download. Nothing is ever sent over the network.
